The Golden Rules of AI Use at Baylor

Before you open any AI tool, know these three principles. They apply across every course, every assignment, and every tool covered in this guide.

★ Start Here Every Time

Three things to always do

Rule 01

Check your syllabus first

Every instructor sets their own AI policy. Before using any AI tool on any assignment, read the syllabus. When in doubt, ask your instructor — before you submit, not after.

Rule 02

Verify everything

AI models make things up — confidently. Every fact, citation, and claim an AI produces must be checked against a real source before you use it in your work.

Rule 03

Document your process

If your instructor permits AI use, keep a record of the prompts you used and how you revised the output. Transparency is a form of academic honesty.

Baylor's default position

Baylor's Honor Code prohibits submitting AI-generated work as your own without authorization. Unauthorized AI use is treated the same as plagiarism. The default is prohibition — only use AI on an assignment if your instructor has explicitly said you may.

Purpose

This guide serves as a starting point for Baylor students thinking about using generative AI tools in their coursework and research.

It provides resources for understanding how generative AI works, how to use it effectively and honestly, and how to evaluate what it produces. It also covers Baylor's academic integrity policies, how to cite generative AI tools, and which tools — provided by the library or freely available — are best suited for different tasks.

We also offer an Instructor Guide to Generative AI for faculty and graduate teaching assistants as well as a guide on Integrating AI into your Research.

Audience

This library guide is part of our “spotlight on the disciplines” literacy tier. In this tier you will be introduced to advanced academic research skills.

This particular guide is aimed at Baylor students: undergraduates and graduate students at all levels of familiarity with generative AI.

We also offer an Instructor Guide to Generative AI, a Guide to Integrating AI Tools into your Research, and FAQs about generative AI.
