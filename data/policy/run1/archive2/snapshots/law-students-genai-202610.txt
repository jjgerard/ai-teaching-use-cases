The AI Sustainability Group

Thinking Seriously About AI – Together

Why it matters

AI is changing how we teach, learn and work. Getting that right – responsibly, sustainably and in a way that works for everyone – takes more than technology. It takes people asking the right questions.

What we do

The ULaw AI Sustainability Group brings together staff and students from across the University to explore what responsible AI use looks like in practice. We share thinking, surface emerging issues and feed into the University's wider approach to AI.

Why every voice counts

Our Group draws on perspectives from teaching technology, law, business and – crucially – our students. Three Student Representatives sit at the heart of the Group, because the people AI affects most should have a say in how we use it.

Who we are

Formed in 2024, we are a cross-University group of staff and students committed to making sure AI at ULaw works for everyone.

Training and Development: For Colleagues and Students

Building AI Confidence Across ULaw

AI fluency is not optional: it is a graduate attribute and a professional necessity. That is why we have developed the ULaw AI Digital Pathway: a structured, three-level programme of training for both colleagues and students.

The AI Digital Pathway

Level One: Foundations (Mandatory for all colleagues and students by September 2026)

- AI Essentials for All (Digital Education Council)

- How to Use AI Responsibly (Colleagues)

- Introduction to Ethical Uses of AI at ULaw (Students)

- Sustainability in AI (launching soon)

Level Two: Role-Specific Development

- Navigating AI: A Practical Guide for Colleagues at ULaw (launching soon)

- Navigating AI: A Practical Guide for Students at ULaw

- JISC Digital Skills in AI and Generative AI Resources

- Law: Harvey AI for Legal Basics; Luminance Academic Programme

- Business: AI Tools for Business Research

Level Three: AI Champion

- Digital Education Council Advanced Training (launching soon)

AI Fluency as a Graduate Attribute

Preparing You for the Professions of Tomorrow

The professions our graduates enter are already being reshaped by AI. At ULaw, we are committed to ensuring that every graduate leaves with:

- The ability to critically evaluate AI outputs

- The confidence to use AI tools responsibly in professional practice

- A deep understanding of the ethical, legal, and societal implications of AI

- The adaptability to thrive in a landscape that will continue to evolve

AI fluency is not a bolt-on. It is woven into the fabric of a ULaw education.
