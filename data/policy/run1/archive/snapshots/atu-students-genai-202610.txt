Academic Integrity

Welcome to the Academic Integrity Hub at ATU

At Atlantic Technological University (ATU), we are dedicated to fostering a culture of honesty, trust, and ethical behaviours in all aspects of academic life. Our Academic Integrity Hub is a comprehensive resource designed to support academic staff, professional support staff and students in understanding and upholding the highest standards of academic integrity.

What We Offer

For Students:

- Academic Integrity Policies: Understand the ATU’s policies on academic integrity, including guidelines on plagiarism, referencing, and the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence tools.

- Student Resources: Access tools and materials to help you maintain academic integrity, including referencing guides, plagiarism detection tools, study skills resources, and more.

- Workshops and Training: Participate in workshops and training sessions designed to educate and support you in your academic journey.

- Assessment Support: Find tips and guidelines for preparing assessments that meet the highest standards of academic integrity.

- Using Artificial Intelligence Tools Responsibly: Learn how to use Artificial Intelligence tools responsibly in your academic work, including case studies and ethical guidelines.

For Staff:

- Teaching Resources: Explore materials and guidelines on incorporating academic integrity into your teaching and assessment practices.

- Policy Implementation: Find support for implementing and upholding academic integrity policies within your courses.

- Workshops and Training: Engage in professional development opportunities that focus on promoting academic integrity in higher education.

- Artificial Intelligence Integration: Discover best practices for integrating Artificial Intelligence tools into your teaching ethically and responsibly.
