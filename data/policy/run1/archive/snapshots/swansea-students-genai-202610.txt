As artificial intelligence becomes an integral part of the academic and professional world, it is vital that students are not only aware of these emerging technologies but also empowered to engage with them critically and creatively.

This section of the AI Framework is designed specifically for students—offering clear explanations, ethical insights, and practical tools to help you understand, apply, and reflect on the role of AI in your learning journey and beyond.

Responsible and Ethical Use

Artificial Intelligence is becoming an integral part of education, offering new ways to learn, explore, and create. As students, engaging with AI means more than just using tools—it involves understanding the broader implications of how AI shapes knowledge, communication, and decision-making. Responsible and ethical use of AI is about developing digital literacy, respecting academic values, and preparing to navigate a world where AI is increasingly present in everyday life.

Legal, Regulatory and Policy Awareness and Compliance

Baseline

Apply University policies on data protection, privacy, and academic integrity when selecting and using approved academic work, ensuring responsible and ethical practice.

Established

Reference and acknowledge policy and regulatory frameworks in interactions with peers relating to AI use, such as group projects.

Innovative

Contribute to institutional and/or student-led initiatives relating to AI governance.

Using AI through a Critical Lens

Baseline

Critically assess the limitations of generative AI outputs by identifying potential biases and inaccuracies, and edit the content using informed judgement before incorporating it into academic work.

Established

Identify and use criteria for evaluating the accuracy of AI output and address any issues identified.

Innovative

Critically analyse the effectiveness of AI in performing and/or supporting certain tasks in both academia and authentic professional contexts.

Acknowledging and Referencing AI Use

Baseline

Apply appropriate referencing techniques to acknowledge the use of AI generated content in academic work, including accurate use of in-text citations in line with institutional academic integrity guidelines.

Established

Apply institutional accepted referencing standards for AI tools, including in-text citations, reference lists, and university declaration statements.

Innovative

Consistently acknowledge and reflect upon AI use in academic contexts, even where not specifically required.

Core A.I Literacy

As AI becomes more embedded in education and everyday life, developing a foundational understanding of how it works is essential. Core AI literacy empowers students to engage critically and creatively with AI tools, understand their capabilities and limitations, and reflect on the broader social and ethical implications of their use, helping students become confident and responsible users in both academic and real-world contexts.

Understanding AI and its functions

Baseline

Identify and compare the core functions of commonly used AI tools, selecting appropriate tools for specific academic or professional tasks.Identify and compare the key functions of commonly used AI tools, and select appropriate tools to support specific academic tasks or problem-solving activities.

Established

Access institutional tools and use their basic functionality.

Innovative

Purposefully select and combine different AI tools in more advanced contexts.

Being aware of the global role and impact of AI

Baseline

Established

Engage with developments in the field, commentary and literature to remain up to date on the global role and impact of AI.

Innovative

Develop and implement strategies for making informed choices on AI use.

Role-Specific A.I Literacy

As AI becomes more integrated into academic life, students are expected to develop ethical and effective use of these technologies to maintain academic integrity when using AI, recognise how AI can be applied within specific disciplines, and explore ways it can enhance personal learning. Developing these competencies helps students use AI as a tool for growth, creativity, and deeper engagement with their subject areas, while upholding the values of responsible scholarship.

Demonstrating Academic Integrity

Baseline

Recognise the risks of over-reliance on AI including the devaluation of learning opportunities at University

Established

Follow assessment instructions around permitted AI use and disclosure

Innovative

Influence and support peers in responsible, compliant and purposeful use of AI in assessment

Using AI in specific disciplines

Baseline

Explore how AI tools and applications are influencing professional practices within your field of study, and explain their potential implications for future developments.

Established

Use AI for discipline-specific learning activities and tasks.

Innovative

Analyse how AI tools can be used strategically alongside human input to improve the effectiveness of professional practice in your area of study.

Enhancing learning with AI

Baseline

Use basic functions of AI tools to build knowledge and address misconceptions and/or gaps in understanding.

Established

Use AI tools to develop and use study resources including summaries, key points for revision and self-assessments.

Innovative

Share effective and responsible AI-supported study practices with others.
