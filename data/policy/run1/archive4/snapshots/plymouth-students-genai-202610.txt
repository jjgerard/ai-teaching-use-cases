University GenAI Principles (2025-26)

Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is reshaping Higher Education. These technologies present both challenges and opportunities across teaching, learning, assessment, and research. The University of Plymouth recognises the importance of engaging critically, ethically, and constructively with GenAI to prepare staff and students for an AI-informed future.
