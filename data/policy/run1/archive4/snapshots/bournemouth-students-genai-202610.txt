Using this Guide

Book onto an Academic skills workshop or work through recordings of Academic skills workshops in the tabs above at your own pace.

Take our Academic skills audit to find resources appropriate to your individual learning needs.

If you can't find the workshop you need, please check the tabs above for workshop recordings, explore the resources in the Academic Skills Hub or contact us at academicskills@bournemouth.ac.uk.
