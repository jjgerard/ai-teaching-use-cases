Draft, first tier only. documents from a stratified sample of institutions, all coded by one model. Coders flagged about 28 as thin or not institution-wide student guidance: hub pages, library guides, whole misconduct procedures, staff or governance papers. Use "Rule-setting documents only" below before reading any pattern. Nothing here is a prevalence estimate.

How to read this

  • Coverage is by a curated frame of institutions and a seeded stratified sample, not by search. Twelve found documents could not be archived (bot walls, JavaScript-only pages, robots.txt) and are not coded.
  • Two coders, both the same model, coded 5 documents independently. They agreed on the kind of answer for 91% of cells (98% leaving out eight staff and teaching variables) and on the exact value in 87% of the 84 cells where both gave one. The weakest variables were default when silent and how misconduct is framed. This is optimistic and small. Human double-coding is still needed before any claim about how common something is.
  • Hub pages cannot be registered with an empty domain list, so some of their assessment variables show as silent where "does not apply" would be more accurate.
  • The pairing table runs hundreds of comparisons on a few dozen documents, so some small p-values appear by chance. Only pairs that survive a false-discovery correction are flagged, and even those need checking: two variables in a pair can be coded from the same sentence or by the same decision rule.
  • Codebook version . Rows coded under a different version are rejected, so a revised vocabulary means re-coding.
