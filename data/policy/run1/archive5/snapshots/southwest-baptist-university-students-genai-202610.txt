Theology, Preaching & Plagiarism

If you plan to utilize an article for a paper, and you are unable to verify the existence of the references listed in the article, this can be an indicator that the article is A.I. generated. Use of such an article in your bibliography/list of references could be detrimental to your own work, so proceed with caution.
