Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has changed immeasurably over the last few years and continues to develop rapidly. This guide explains how you can use it appropriately and transparently in your studies and introduces some tools that can help you.

We have categorised our guidance into the subheadings in the left hand menu. The Digital Skills Hub also contains support for using AI, including LinkedIn Learning training. There is further information and University guidance on using Generative AI on The Hub (for staff) and MyCranfield (for students).

We have also created a reading list of guidance and other useful material about AI.

We will do our best to keep the information you can find here up to date but AI is a fast-moving area. If you can’t find what you are looking for, if you feel anything on these pages is incomplete or unclear, or if you want to make us aware of any useful tools or resources we haven't mentioned, please email us.
