Using AI responsibly
AI is one of many tools available to support work and study. If you choose to use it, we want you to feel confident making informed choices about when it is appropriate, which tool to use, what information you can share and how to check the results.
Before using an AI tool, ask yourself these four questions:
Is AI appropriate?
 Do I actually need to use AI for this task?
Am I using the right tool?
 Check the AI Catalogue for approved tools.
Can I put this information into AI?
 Check the Information Classification Scheme and relevant guidance.
Have I checked the result?
 AI can make mistakes. Use your own judgement and check outputs carefully.
Guidance for you
Training and support
At Exeter, we’re learning together, building our confidence and skills so we can use AI responsibly, creatively and effectively. Everyone is at a different stage, so a range of training and support is available to help you explore AI at a pace that works for you.
Our support will continue to develop as AI evolves. If there’s something you’d like to explore or can’t find here, get in touch with your relevant Community of Practice or contact

Our approach
Our AI Policy
The 
University of Exeter Artificial Intelligence policy
 sets out clear principles for using AI at Exeter. It supports innovation while ensuring fairness, transparency and compliance with data protection and ethical standards.
All colleagues, students and researchers should refer to the policy when using, developing or procuring an AI tool for University purposes, from drafting content to analysing data.
For research colleagues:
 Responsible AI use is vital for maintaining research integrity. For more information read our guidance on
Responsible Use of AI in research
.
Our Enabling AI Strategy
The University of Exeter’s 
Enabling AI Strategy
 sets out our vision to embed Artificial Intelligence responsibly and sustainably across education, research and professional services.
QS Responsible AI Consortium
We are a member of the 
QS Responsible AI Consortium
, working with universities and partners internationally to advance the responsible, ethical and sustainable use of AI in higher education and help realise its potential for the public good.
