Responsible AI Use

Using AI responsibly means using AI tools to support your learning while meeting assessment requirements and academic integrity expectations.

Using AI responsibly is now an important part of university study and professional practice.

AI can support brainstorming, research, productivity, and communication, but it should not replace your own thinking, analysis, or academic judgement.

You remain responsible for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of all work you submit, including work created with AI assistance.

1. Check assessment requirements

Before using AI, make sure you:

- read your Subject Outline and assessment instructions

- check guidance in Canvas

- ask your Learning Facilitator if you are unsure

Using AI where it is not permitted, or failing to acknowledge AI use where required, may be treated as academic misconduct.

2. Acknowledge AI use

When using AI:

- identify which AI tools you used

- explain how they supported your work

- keep records of prompts or outputs so you can show how you used AI if asked

See:

- Acknowledging AI Use

- Statement of Acknowledgement

- Documenting AI

3. Use AI critically and ethically

AI-generated content is not always reliable and can sometimes be inaccurate, biased, incomplete, or misleading.

Always:

- verify information using credible academic sources

- review AI-generated content carefully

- apply your own judgement and analysis

Protect your privacy and university data.

Do not upload the following into unapproved or public AI tools:

- assessment briefs

- your own assignment drafts in unapproved tools

- personal information

- unpublished research

- university materials

Ask yourself: Could I explain this work in my own words without AI?

See Torrens AI Tools for approved platforms and guidance.

Key principles

Be transparent

Clearly state if, when, and how AI tools were used.

Think critically

Review and evaluate AI-generated outputs carefully.

Submit your own work

Your work must demonstrate your own understanding, analysis, and academic contribution.

Acknowledge AI use

Acknowledge and reference AI use in accordance with university requirements.
