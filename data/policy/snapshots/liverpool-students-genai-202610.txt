Students: AI for your studies

These resources will help you identify your strengths when using the technology and where you might need some development, and will ultimately help you get the most out of the technology.

Online tutorials - using AI effectively

- Guidance around the use of GAI at the University of Liverpool – this tutorial explores some of the ways you can use AI tools appropriately to assist with your studies, along with how to avoid the uses which are unacceptable

- Thinking Critically When Using generative AI - AI tools can provide information which is incorrect or biased, so it is important to apply your critical thinking skills to check the reliability of outputs

- Developing effective prompts – this tutorial provides a step by step process to create and evaluate prompts to ensure they are effective and provide the best possible output

- AI tools for research – AI tools can be used to enhance and support traditional research methods and save time throughout various stages of the research process. This tutorial explores some of the tools which could be useful

- Data analysis with AI - AI tools can help you organise, visualise and analyse data more efficiently. This tutorial explores a range of AI-powered approaches and tools, along with important considerations around data protection, security and responsible use.

- Cite them Right guides on referencing GAI tools - a collection of guidance hosted by Cite Them Right on how to cite/reference generative AI under the different referencing systems. You can log in with your university credentials.

Jisc Discovery tool

Use these resources to reflect on your current digital literacies and skills and identify some areas for development.

Jisc - Artificial Intelligence question set (login required): a self-assessment tool that asks you reflective questions about your digital capabilities around artificial intelligence and provides you with insights and reports on your skills and where to focus your attention to develop further. The questions are non-judgemental and open-ended, and your responses are private to you.

Watch the video to find out more about the Jisc AI question set.

You can find further resources on the KnowHow AI website.
