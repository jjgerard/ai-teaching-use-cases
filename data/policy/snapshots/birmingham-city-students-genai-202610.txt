AI Guidelines: Welcome

This guide outlines the BCU Student AI Guidelines 2026-27.

What is GenAI?

Generative AI (GenAI) refers to artificial intelligence that can generate new text, images, videos, and audio from a user prompt. Common examples include ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and DeepSeek.

The default GenAI tool at BCU is Microsoft Copilot, for which you have free access via your BCU email address: https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat. Do not use your BCU email address to sign up for third-party AI tools.

What’s BCU’s position on GenAI?

BCU embraces a positive approach to GenAI and encourages students to use it in critical and responsible ways to support their learning, which are explained below. However, the rules around GenAI use may differ between assessments. Your assessment or exam brief will explain the AI usage level for that specific task (Option A – AI use allowed with conditions; Option B – AI not allowed; or Option C – AI required). Always check your assessment brief and exam instructions carefully. If you are not sure, please check with your module tutor.
