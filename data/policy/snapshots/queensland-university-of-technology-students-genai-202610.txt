AI guides for undergraduate students and researchers and referencing and acknowledging Gen AI in your assessments

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is rapidly becoming an integral part of our daily lives, work, learning and study. As GenAI uptake accelerates, mastering the effective, responsible and ethical use of this technology is essential for your academic journey and future career.

This guide is a terrific starting point for students: Generative AI - an introductory guide for QUT students

This next guide for a students focusses on issues around the use of Generative AI in assignments. Your lecturers will also know how they would like you to use GenAI (or not). Learning with Generative AI

QUT Library Guide on Generative AI in Research and Teaching: This is a great introduction to the world of generative AI, and is focused on its applications in research and teaching. Definitely worth a look if you are moving into the research world.

QUT Cite Write also has a section on referencing AI in each referencing style. Here's the page on referencing AI in APA7

There's also a very thorough QUT Library guide (click below) on how best to acknowledge your use of AI in your work:

Check out QUT Library's collection of AI texts. A growing collection of new books on the topic. Stay up to date!

Research students, have a look at QUT's responsible use of AI in research (self-paced module or scheduled workshop) Learn how AI can help and hinder your research writing by exploring relevant scenarios. This self-paced online module provides examples of how Artificial Intelligence tools could be utilised by HDR students in your research processes and poses questions about how to do so in an ethical and responsible manner.
