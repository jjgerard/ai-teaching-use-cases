AI at Huston-Tillotson University

At HT, we approach artificial intelligence the way we approach everything: with intention, equity, and an eye toward who gets to shape the future. AI is already part of how our students learn and our faculty teach. Our job is to make sure that happens responsibly, transparently, and in service of HT's mission.
